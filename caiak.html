Conference, AI and Kayaking

CAIAK was founded by Jan Karaś in 2024 and has been organized ever since by the team of KNSI Czarna Magia. What started as a small gathering has grown into an annual event bringing together AI science clubs from across Poland, giving students a space to meet, share their work, and build lasting connections in the unique surroundings of Warmian nature.

Participants

During the conference, members of AI science clubs from Poland and Europe meet to present their projects, listen to industry experts and exchange experiences. The first part consists of lectures and discussion panels, and the second part sees participants go on a kayaking trip, which fosters less formal conversations and making new contacts.

Nationwide community

For us, CAIAK is not just a conference, but a space to create cooperation between young AI enthusiasts, which results in new initiatives and projects. Each edition attracts more and more participants, and we make sure that every time it is an event full of substantive knowledge and valuable conversations.
